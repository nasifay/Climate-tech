Climate Game privacy information

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This page explains what the multiplayer Climate Game stores and how to manage it. The game is educational entertainment; its Eco-Score is not a measurement of real-world emissions or environmental impact.

Information used

  • Account email: used by Supabase Auth to create and sign in to your account, and to send account-confirmation messages. It is not displayed in public rankings.
  • Display name: the name shown in your profile and, only if you opt in, in the public leaderboard.
  • Game results: server-validated score events, completed levels, and timestamps used to maintain your score and calculate daily and weekly rankings.
  • Technical data: the hosting, authentication, and database services may process network and service logs needed to operate and protect the game. Their own privacy terms also apply.

The game does not ask for your phone number or Telegram contact details. Do not put private information in your display name.

Leaderboard choice

Leaderboard participation is optional. You may change the “Show me in public leaderboards” setting in your profile at any time. Public rankings contain your chosen display name and scores, not your email address or account ID.

Storage and deletion

Supabase is used for sign-in and durable game data; Vercel hosts the application and API. An authenticated player can use Delete Account and Score Data in the profile settings to delete the account, player record, score history, and leaderboard entry. This deletion cannot be undone. Some infrastructure logs may remain for the period required by the hosting provider or applicable law.

Children and consent

Use the game only if you are permitted to create an online account under the laws that apply where you live. If you need a parent or guardian’s permission, obtain it before registering or choosing public leaderboard display.

Questions or privacy requests

Contact the project organizer through the official channel where you received the Climate Game link. Before a broad public launch, the organizer should add a monitored privacy/support email and any locally required legal/business information here.
